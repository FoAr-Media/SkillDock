# SkillDock brand guidelines

- **Name:** SkillDock. **Tagline:** Your AI ecosystem. One control center. **Secondary:** Dock your skills. Unlock your AI.
- **Description:** The open-source desktop control center for AI skills, MCP servers, plugins and agent workflows.
- **Principles:** Unified, Open, Modular, Empowering, Transparent, Accessible.
- **Voice:** plain verbs, sentence case, the same name for an action across its flow, errors say what happened and what to do. Not an official Anthropic app; never imply otherwise.
- **Assets:** supplied rasters live in `assets/brand/`; shipped derivatives and their status are in `ASSET_REGISTRY.md`. Use the registry (`shared/brand`), never raw paths. Keep aspect ratio; do not recolour or add effects. The masters are opaque PNGs, not vectors: ask for transparent/vector masters for production.
- **Colour:** primary #7C3AED, light #A78BFA, hover #6D28D9, accent #22D3EE; neutrals and status colours are in `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.
- **Gradients and glow:** brand moments only (logo, welcome screen). The working UI is neutral and precise.
